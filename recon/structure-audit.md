# Structure Audit — YC & FOSS United, for DoingThingsAI

Inspo sites named by Chandru: Y Combinator, FOSS United. Both audited via live fetch, 2026-09-28.
This is structure only. Visual design comes later.

## What YC does that DoingThingsAI should steal

- **Before/after proof, not a logo grid.** YC's lead device isn't a card wall — it's one founder, one photo, one sentence naming a real outcome. Maps directly to "Products first, success stories, if they can you can too."
- **One CTA word, everywhere.** YC uses "Apply" in nav, hero, mid-page, footer. No variation. DoingThingsAI should pick one word (e.g. "RSVP" or "Join the Build") and repeat it, not rotate synonyms.
- **Team framed as alumni, not staff.** YC's partner bios open with "was a founder first." DoingThingsAI's People/Builders Directory should do the same — organizers shown as builders who shipped, not admins.
- **Batch-code tagging.** YC tags founders with a batch (W09). DoingThingsAI already has this organically — "Build & Blend 15." Use edition numbers as the cohort marker across the site.

## What YC does that DoingThingsAI should skip

- **Deep mega-nav** (6 dropdowns, dozens of sub-links). Wrong scale for a 180-follower, single-city community. Sticky note already wants a flat list — keep it flat.
- **Dollar-figure/deadline apply page.** No funding program here. Not applicable.
- **Logo wall of recognized brands.** Recon found zero confirmed brand partners. Don't build this section until real names exist — an empty or placeholder logo wall reads as fake.

## What FOSS United does that DoingThingsAI should steal

- **Numbers-as-hero-copy.** "Over 401 people, United by FOSS" — a real, specific, live number used as headline copy, not buried in a stats block. DoingThingsAI can do this once a real member count exists (see gap below).
- **Tone.** FOSS United's copy is the closer match to "funky, bold, crazy, jovial" — first-person plural, alliterative, occasionally self-deprecating ("one-person-army"), not corporate. YC's tone is polished-corporate by comparison. Lean FOSS United for voice.
- **Candid status badges.** FOSS United marks dead chapters "Inactive"/"Defunct" right on the card instead of hiding them. Same honesty instinct fits a small community — don't inflate.
- **Event timeline split: Upcoming / Completed.** Clean pattern for the "Past events" nav item, and it already matches how Build & Blend editions are numbered.

## What FOSS United does that DoingThingsAI should skip

- **Financial/legal transparency page** (CIN, PAN, TAN, bank details). Only applies if DoingThingsAI is a registered nonprofit — unconfirmed. Do not build this section without confirming legal structure.
- **Nav that fans "Grants" into 6 sub-pages.** Same over-scale problem as YC's mega-nav. Skip.
- **No FAQ page exists on FOSS United itself**, despite being a plausible nav item elsewhere on their site. Not a pattern to copy — but Chandru's own sticky note asks for FAQs, so keep it on our list; it's his call, not inspo-driven.

## Content gaps found during recon (blockers, not decisions)

These sections are requested (sticky note / meeting) but recon found **no real data** to fill them:
- **Products we built** — no named products/projects found anywhere. This is the homepage lead section per the second meeting. Needs a real list from Chandru before V0 can show real content here.
- **Metrics** — no confirmed member count. Sticky note says "40-200" and "2000+"; Instagram shows 180 followers. These don't reconcile. Needs one real number.
- **Brands we work with** — zero found.
- **Collaboration Requests / People / Builders Directory** — only two named humans found (Chandrasekar J, Shankar K).

Recommendation: build V0 structure now (placeholders clearly marked), do not fabricate content to fill these.
