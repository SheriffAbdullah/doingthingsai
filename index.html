FAQs

Do I need a finished product to attend?

No. Founders, developers, designers, marketers, students, professionals, and AI enthusiasts are all welcome. No finished product or startup required.

How much does it cost?

Build & Blend sessions are typically ₹250, which covers snacks and refreshments.

How do I RSVP?

RSVP through our Luma page. Registration needs approval, and once you're in, payment is confirmed over WhatsApp.

Is it really closed-door?

Yes, on purpose. Most editions run around 8 people. We'd rather have a room full of people actually building than a big audience watching.
